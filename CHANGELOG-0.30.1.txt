OOTP Front Office 0.30.1 — everything since 0.30.0

Fixed — The availability column on the pitching page is readable in light mode. The three pills carried fixed colours picked for a dark background, painted over a tint of themselves, so on a light screen the text and its own backing were nearly the same brightness — measured against a table row they came to 1.4, 1.4 and 2.1 where the accessibility floor is 4.5. They are drawn from the theme now, which knows how to flip a colour between modes, and they have lost their fill: the border carries the pill shape and every tint tried cost contrast the colour could not spare. On a light screen they now read 4.9, 5.3 and 5.0. The injury tag and the chat's error line carried the same hardcoded red and got the same treatment.

Fixed — The contrast check that is meant to catch this could not have. It measured colours against the page background, but a table paints itself a shade darker than the page — so it was checking a surface these are never drawn on, which by itself was the difference between a pass and a fail. And the amber had no entry in the palette at all, so there was nothing for it to look at even in principle. Both are closed, the light-mode greens and reds are deep enough to clear a table row rather than just the page, and the arithmetic now runs in the test suite as well, so a palette that fails cannot ship by somebody forgetting to run the script.
