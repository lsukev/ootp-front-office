OOTP Front Office 0.16.1 — everything since 0.16.0

Fixed — Storylines was blending a call-up's minor-league numbers into his major-league line. A reader reported a storyline setting his closer against another reliever where one line was major-league and the other Triple-A, presented as like for like. A career line carries a row per level a man has played at this year, and the query summed them: on the club this was reproduced against, one pitcher read as a 4.91 ERA that was neither his 5.89 in the majors nor his 3.34 in Triple-A, and four pitchers and two batters on that one roster were wrong. The club's own level is now filtered for, and named in what the model is given, since the prospects alongside carry minor-league lines by their nature.

Fixed — Players with arbitration years left are no longer counted as money coming off the books. A reader pointed out that an expiring deal and a departing player are different things, and asked for both. On the club this was checked against the old single figure read 36 players and $74M of relief, of which only 8 players and $33M actually leave — the other $41M is 28 men you still hold, whose salaries are about to rise rather than vanish. Payroll & Budget now shows two panels: who reaches free agency, and whose deal ends without them going anywhere, each marked with which arbitration trip it would be. The Contracts page has drawn this distinction for some time; the two now share one piece of reasoning rather than each having their own.
