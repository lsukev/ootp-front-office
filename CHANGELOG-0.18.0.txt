OOTP Front Office 0.18.0 — everything since 0.17.1

New — Two-way players now count in both halves of the club. The roster was split on a man's single listed position, so somebody listed at designated hitter had his innings vanish from the Pitching Staff page — eighty-six of Ohtani's, in the save this was found in. He appears on both sides now, along with the twelve others in that league, and pitchers who genuinely hit are among the bats. Judged on what a man has actually done rather than his ratings, because a threshold on ratings would read well on one scale and be nonsense on another, while innings and plate appearances mean the same thing in every save. Twenty innings and a hundred plate appearances: enough to be a job rather than an outfielder mopping up a blowout.

New — A season split between two clubs is broken out. Where somebody arrived mid-season the season line has always covered both clubs, which is the right total, but the split was not on offer anywhere the assistants looked — so asked how a new arrival was hitting, they gave the year and stopped. Player search now carries the per-club breakdown and the answer leads with the season and then what he has done since arriving.

Fixed — Team logos no longer stick when you switch saves. They are served by team id with a day's cache on them, and team ids repeat between saves, so the browser went on showing one league's badges against another league's clubs. Each save now gets its own cache entry.

Fixed — Ratings are drawn on the scale your save actually uses. OOTP lets you pick 20-80, 1-20, 1-10, 2-8 or 1-5 and the export carries whichever you chose, but the bars divided by eighty regardless — so on the 1-to-5 scale a 5, the top of the scale, drew as a sliver at six per cent of the width. Nothing in the export states the scale, so it is now read off the data. The increments-of-five setting is affected too: it belongs to the 20-80 scale, and would have turned a 3 into a 5 and a 2 into nothing, so it now applies only where the scale can carry it.
