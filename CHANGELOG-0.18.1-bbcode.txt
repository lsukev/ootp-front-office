[b][size=4]OOTP Front Office 0.18.1 — everything since 0.18.0[/size][/b]
[list]
[*][b]Fixed[/b] — Pitchers on the injured list are no longer described as rested. A user sent his bullpen with two injured men in it, one tagged IL-60 with six weeks left to run, and beside that tag, in green, "Rested 18d". The availability column was measuring days since the last appearance, so a man who had not pitched in weeks came out beautifully fresh — the opposite of the answer, on a page whose only question is who can throw tonight. Health settles it before workload is read at all, and the heading's count of who is limited or unavailable counts him too, which it also was not doing. Day-to-day men are unchanged: OOTP will let you use them, so that stays your call.
[*][b]New[/b] — Injured arms are hidden from the bullpen, and a control in the heading brings them back. Off by default, with the number being held back always on screen — "2 on the IL — show" — so nothing goes missing quietly. The choice is remembered in your settings rather than in the browser, so it survives an update. Starting Depth keeps its injured starters whatever the setting says; it is the only place they appear.
[/list]
