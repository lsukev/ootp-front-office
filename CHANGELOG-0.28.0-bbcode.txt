[*][b]New[/b] — The farm page names the other half of a call-up. It ranked minor leaguers against their own level and stopped there, so it never once looked at your major-league club — on my own save that meant fifteen call-ups recommended when two of them improve anything. Every man now shows who would come off the big club to make room, or who is standing in his way. Where everybody at his position in the majors is graded above him the verdict reads blocked instead of promote: he has earned it where he is, he stays on the page because an injury or a trade can make him matter tomorrow, but the app stops calling for a move that costs you something.
The comparison runs on OOTP's Overall grade rather than the season lines, and that is the only reason it can be made at all — a .900 OPS in Double-A and a .900 OPS in the majors are not the same achievement, while the grade is scouted current ability and means the same thing at every level. The bar is beating the weakest man at the spot, which is the least that can be asked of somebody being given a roster place rather than a starting job. Two things it does not do, and says so on the page: it reads his listed position only, so a shortstop blocked by a shortstop may still have a home at second, and where either grade is missing it offers no verdict rather than a guess. The staff chat is told the same thing, so it stops recommending the call-ups the page no longer does.
