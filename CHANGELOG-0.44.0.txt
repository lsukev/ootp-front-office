OOTP Front Office 0.44.0 — everything since 0.43.0

New — The Paper and the League Recap cover the week where your league sims a week. Online leagues that run a seven-day schedule were getting an issue about the seventh day of it: six days of races, deals and form were simply not in the writing, and nothing on the page admitted it. The app works the stretch out for itself from how far apart your exports are, so a save you sim a day at a time reads exactly as it always has, and nobody has to tell the app what kind of league this is. Every result and every deal now carries its own date, so a week reads in order. Ten days is the most one issue holds — come back after two months and you get the recent stretch, with the writing told it is a clipped one.

New — The Lineup page's PA, OPS, OPS+ and wRC+ follow the vs-RHP / vs-LHP toggle. Off Value always did; the production columns did not, so a card built to face a left-hander was showing you a season of mostly right-handed pitching beside it. The split plate appearances are right there in the column, because a .400 line over forty trips is worth reading as forty trips. The rates are measured against the league in that same split, not against the league as a whole. The production sort follows too, pulled toward the man's own full-season line rather than toward league average — fifteen at-bats against left-handers should not demote a 7-WAR bat. WAR stays full-season, which is what it counts.

Fixed — Hide overall and potential now hides them everywhere. 0.43.0 honoured the setting on the roster, the farm system, the depth chart and the player card, and leaked it on the two places you meet most. The hover card wrote "OA 57→63" over every name on every page. The draft board — three grade columns, a ceiling filter and a sort on potential — did not know the setting existed; its grade columns and the ceiling filter now go, and the board falls back to its own ranking. The per-position defensive grades stay, as they always have.

Fixed — The draft board's "nothing matches those filters" row spanned ten columns against eleven, which it has done since the Taken box arrived.
