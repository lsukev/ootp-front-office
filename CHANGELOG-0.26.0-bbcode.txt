[list]
[*][b]New[/b] — The farm page can tell you somebody should go down. It ranked minor leaguers by promotion signal and stopped there, so a twenty-four-year-old hitting .180 at Single-A drew the same blank as a nineteen-year-old holding his own. Demote asks a bigger gap than promote does, because sending a man down is the easier call to get wrong, and it asks that he not be young for the level — nineteen struggling at Double-A is on schedule, twenty-six struggling at Single-A is not the same sentence. It is never offered for the lowest club in your organisation, since there is nowhere to send him, and that bottom is read from your own affiliates rather than assumed. Each one shows the case against him beside the verdict: his line, the level average, and how much older than the level he is.
[*][b]Fixed[/b] — The promote and watch badges are readable in light mode. Their backgrounds were fixed dark colours meant for a dark table while the text came from the theme, which light mode turns dark — dark on dark, and a reader could not make out either one. Every tinted plate tried cost contrast the accent could not spare, so the badges have no fill at all now: the colour and the border carry them, and all three inherit the contrast the theme check already guarantees. They read at 5.0 or better in light mode and 5.9 or better in dark.
