[*][b]New[/b] — A daily recap of the whole league, under League → Daily Recap. Every other page here is about your club; this is the morning paper. One short paragraph per division saying who won and what it did to the race, then a handful of notes on the season's leaders. It covers the last day the league actually played rather than the save's today, since a save exported in the morning has not played today's games — and if you read one after simming on, it tells you which day it covers and offers to write the new one. Divisions where nobody played are left out rather than padded with a sentence about nothing happening. Turn on "generate after import" in Settings and a fresh one is written every time you re-export, which is the point of a daily thing.
Two small things it is careful about, because a wrong one reads as fact. A man level at the top of a category is described as sharing the lead, not leading it — the first draft wrote "leads with 13 home runs" on a day three men had thirteen. And the leaders come from this season's own lines rather than OOTP's league-leader table, which is filled in at the end of a season and in May still holds last year's winners.
