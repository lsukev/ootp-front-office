[b][size=4]OOTP Front Office 0.38.3 — everything since 0.38.2[/size][/b]
[list]
[*][b]New[/b] — [b]The standings page marks who is in and who is out.[/b] An x beside every club that has reached the postseason, the way OOTP prints it, and an e beside one that can no longer get there. Both are worked out exactly rather than guessed: you are in when nobody left in your division can reach your win total, or when fewer clubs than there are wild cards can; you are out when you can no longer win your division and as many clubs as there are places have already won more games than you can finish with. Where a race is genuinely undecided the page says nothing, which is the honest answer.
[*][b]Fixed[/b] — Negative and zero magic numbers are gone from the standings. The dashboard card stopped printing them in 0.38.2 and the standings page carried on, because the rule lived in only one of the two files that needed it. Both read the same rule now.
[*][b]Fixed[/b] — The standings table no longer draws through its own card border. The table needs 615 pixels for its full line and the cards were being laid out at 430, so it had been overrunning them since the page was written — invisibly, until a highlighted row gave it a background to show. The columns and gutters are tighter, and the cards are sized to what the table actually needs. Three divisions still fit across a wide window.
[/list]
