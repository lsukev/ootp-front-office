[*][b]New[/b] — The dashboard says where you stand for a playoff place. Games back in the division is the wrong question for eleven clubs in fifteen, so a line under the division table now gives the race you are actually in: leading it and the magic number, holding a wild card and how clear of the field, or how far back of the last one and where in the queue. None of it is in the export — team_record carries the division figure and nothing else — so the race is worked out from the standings, but the number of places is read from the save rather than assumed. It runs from none to eight across the leagues of a single export, and a league with none says so instead of inventing a race.
[*][b]New[/b] — The general manager is someone you can talk to. He was reachable only through a trade verdict, which is a narrow way to reach the man whose job the rest of it is. He is on the staff list now and can be put in the Room, and he is the same voice the trade desk answers in — the general manager, or his assistant in a save where you hold that chair yourself. His brief is not the analyst's: Peter weighs value and hands over the number, while the GM weighs it against a manager who wants to win tonight, an owner watching the payroll and a farm he cannot spend twice, then says what he would do. Asked whether to buy or sell five and a half games out, he read the standings, roster, staff, injuries, payroll, farm and schedule before answering.
