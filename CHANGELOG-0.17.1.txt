OOTP Front Office 0.17.1 — everything since 0.17.0

Fixed — The staff no longer call a player a last-year man when arbitration is still to come. A reader noticed the assistants describing players that way and asked whether it was skewing how they were valued. The trade desk and the staff chat were handed years remaining on the contract and nothing else, so a man with two arbitration years left read as one about to reach the market. Every player in a trade and every player dossier now carries a control status — reaching free agency, arbitration and which trip it would be, pre-arbitration, or bound by the reserve clause — and the AI is told plainly that arbitration years are years of control and not to price such a player as a rental. Asked afterwards whether a particular reliever was in his last year, the general manager now answers no, explains that arbitration keeps him another season, and notes his price will rise. Worth saying what was not affected: the value and talent numbers come from OOTP's own ratings and never looked at contract length, so nothing was mis-priced in the totals — it was the wording that was wrong.

Fixed — Player Search now says which level a season line came from. It was already keeping a call-up's busiest stint rather than blending his levels together, but it never marked which level that was, so a Triple-A season belonging to somebody now on the major-league roster read as major-league work. This is the same complaint as the Storylines fix in 0.16.1, in a second place.
