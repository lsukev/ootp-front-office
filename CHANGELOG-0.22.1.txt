OOTP Front Office 0.22.1 — everything since 0.22.0

Fixed — Fielding lines are the ones earned at that club's level. The same fault the batting line had last week was sitting in the block immediately below it: a shortstop on a major-league roster was shown 587 innings and six errors when 28 of those innings and none of the errors were in the majors, the rest being Triple-A added in. It is the more misleading of the two, because a fielding percentage carries no sample size on its face — a batting line at least shows the plate appearances behind it, while ".982 in 587 innings" reads as a settled fact about a man who has barely played. The trade desk now names the level in each fielding line rather than merging them, since a clean glove in the minors is not a clean glove in the majors.

Fixed — The dashboard's streak strip names six players instead of three twice. A hitter on a run is usually on two streaks at once, reaching base every night and hitting in most of those games, and both were being listed — so half the panel was repeating itself. Keeping only the longest would have hidden the second fact rather than fixed the first, and since an on-base streak is the longer by its nature, every chip would have said "on-base" and none would ever have mentioned a man was hitting. Both are kept now and the chip says so. Unassigned signings, whom OOTP parks on the parent club, are also kept off it.
