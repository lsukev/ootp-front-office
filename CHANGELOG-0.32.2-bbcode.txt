[list]
[*][b]Fixed[/b] — Big trades are written out in full. OOTP's own summary stops at 255 characters, so the largest deals arrived cut off mid-sentence and sometimes mid-name — in my save 81 of 232 are exactly that long and 33 break inside a name, which is what leaves a stray bracket on screen. The biggest trade being the one you cannot read is the wrong way round, so where the summary has been cut the sentence is composed here instead, from the columns that carry every player on both sides however long the deal was, with the cash and draft picks the cut-off version was also losing. Summaries that survived intact are still printed exactly as OOTP wrote them.
[*][b]Fixed[/b] — Waiver claims show up, and count as yours. OOTP records a claim with every field on the row set to zero — no club, no player, no league — and the feed was asking for messages in your own league, so the one row that was genuinely yours was the one it discarded. The league filter is gone; on my save that took my own club's moves from ten to thirty. A claim reported to you is counted as yours, since nothing else on the row says whose it was; a free-agent signing elsewhere that merely lands in your messages is not.
