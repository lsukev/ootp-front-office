[b][size=4]OOTP Front Office 0.16.0 — everything since 0.15.0[/size][/b]
[list]
[*][b]New[/b] — Buy, hold or sell, at the top of the dashboard. The verdict, the chance of reaching the postseason, and the few facts that produced it. One number drives it, because the question a deadline poses is not "are we good" but "can we still get there from here" — five games out in May and five games out in September are the same line in the standings and opposite answers, so the games remaining are in the arithmetic rather than in your head. Talent comes from run differential rather than the record: a club outscoring its opponents while losing reads better than its record, and is told so. It will say hold, and often — most clubs most of the time genuinely are on the fence, and a tool that manufactures a decision to look decisive is worse than one that admits the season has not decided yet.
[*][b]New[/b] — The batting order can be built from production instead of talent. OOTP's offensive value is a projection from current ratings, and over the rest of a season a projection beats a third of a season of results, so it stays the default. But a man on a .274 on-base percentage batting cleanup on the strength of his ratings is defensible and still hard to look at. The production sort uses this season's wRC+ regressed toward league average on plate appearances, so a hot twenty at-bats does not lead off and nobody with no at-bats is read as anything but average. The slot priority — three best hitters at 1, 2 and 4 — is unchanged in both.
[*][b]Fixed[/b] — Players from the international complex no longer appear among your major leaguers. OOTP parks a signing nobody has assigned yet on the parent club's roster id with no roster entry at all, and the depth chart grouped on that id — so sixteen-year-olds stood among the starting pitchers. Every club in a save carries a few. They now get a column of their own headed Unassigned rather than being dropped, since they are real players in the organisation. Nobody injured, designated or on waivers is affected: they are all still on a roster and stay where they were.
[/list]
