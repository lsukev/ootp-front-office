OOTP Front Office 0.17.0 — everything since 0.16.1

New — The staff know the season's dates. Asked whether to wait a fortnight before selling, and whether the deadline was in August, the general manager answered that nothing in his tools pinned down the date and he would not guess — honest, and needless, since the save carries it. Opening day, the All-Star game, the amateur draft, the trade deadline, roster expansion, the Rule 5 draft and the opening of international free agency now go to every voice in the building, along with the trade desk and the weekly briefing, each with how many days off it is. Dates already gone are kept and marked as past, because whether the deadline has been and gone is as useful as when it falls. Asked again, he opens with twenty-three days to the deadline, names the date, and builds a plan around it — a bar to clear by a series on the 22nd, and sell before the 29th rather than be caught with nothing arranged.
