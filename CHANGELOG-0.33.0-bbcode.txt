[*][b]New[/b] — Saves where the lower leagues are not affiliated to the top one now work. A reader had exactly that and could not reach any club below the majors: the picker asked for major-league level, and in a universe where the lower leagues stand on their own their clubs are Double-A or Single-A, so they were never offered and every page behind the picker was unreachable. What makes a club an organisation is having nothing above it — no parent club, and no parent league above its league — and that is what the picker asks for now. Where the list holds clubs of more than one level it says which, since that is the only thing telling them apart. On an ordinary affiliated save this is the same thirty clubs it always was, to the man.
Six pages went with it — standings, the dashboard's division table, storylines, the AI's context, free agents and the daily recap — each of which asked for a club's league and then also asked for major-league level. OOTP gives every club in a league that league's own level, so that second half never said anything on a major league and excluded every club of an independent one.
This does not invent a farm system: the prospect and promotion pages have nothing to say about a club with no affiliates under it, which is the right answer rather than a missing one.
