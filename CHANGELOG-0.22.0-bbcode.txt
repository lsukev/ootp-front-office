[b][size=4]OOTP Front Office 0.22.0 — everything since 0.21.1[/size][/b]
[list]
[*][b]Changed[/b] — The staff chat answers shorter. It had been writing essays with headings where a sentence would do, and the reason was in its own instructions: "short messages" was the whole of the brevity rule, and it lost every argument with the five rules below it that each asked for more — cite the numbers, explain the surprising ones, break out the stint, name the man in full. A vague instruction does not beat five concrete ones. There is now a stated ceiling, permission to answer in a single sentence, and a list of what to leave out: restating the question, the men considered and rejected, closing summaries, offers of further help. The rules that ask for detail are bounded to match — the one or two numbers that carry the claim rather than every number read.
[*][b]New[/b] — The staff chat can reach four more parts of the app. It could not see the analysis every other screen already had: asked whether to buy or sell, the question a general manager asks most often, it had the standings and had to reason its way there while the dashboard beside it carried a worked postseason probability. It can now read the dashboard (playoff picture, the buy/hold/sell read and the odds behind it, hot and cold players), the contract page with its extend, re-sign and hold-off recommendations and the season line behind each, the free agent list with the club's holes and what money there is, and roster crunch for 40-man and Rule 5 pressure.
[*][b]Fixed[/b] — The chat was being told a season line is every level added together. That was true until last week and is not now, and an assistant working from a description of how the numbers used to behave will narrate them wrongly rather than merely vaguely. It also carries the same warning about Value percentiles the briefing and trade desk were given — that they count playing time and are not a measure of performance — since it reads the same figures and could make the same claim.
[/list]
