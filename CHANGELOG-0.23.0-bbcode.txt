[*][b]New[/b] — Leagues that run their own high-school and college competitions get a draft board. A reader with that setup opened his to 123 players, not one of whom was in the class his game had published that day, while none of his 296 appeared at all. His export settled it: OOTP does not use the draft-eligible flag in a league of that kind. Where amateurs are free-floating players the game flags them; where they are rostered on school clubs it works eligibility out from the school year when the draft comes round, and the flag stays at zero. So the flag is read where it says something and the school year where it does not — high-school seniors and college upperclassmen, with a career-ending injury taking a man out as OOTP's own screen does. Run against his file, that rule returns 296: exactly what his game published, to the man. Leagues of the ordinary kind are untouched, and the board now says when it has read the class rather than the flag.
[*][b]Fixed[/b] — The 123 players the old board was offering him belonged, every one, to a second league's draft. That is the same scoping added last release, now confirmed against a real save of the kind it was written for rather than reasoned about.
