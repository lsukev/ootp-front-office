[b][size=4]OOTP Front Office 0.13.0 — everything since 0.12.0[/size][/b]
[list]
[*][b]New[/b] — The trade desk can go and look things up. Ask it who could cover shortstop with your incumbent leaving and it reads the affiliates in turn and names them — two at Triple-A with their lines and fielding percentages, two further down marked as not ready — instead of telling you it only has the players in the deal in front of it. It has the same tools the staff chat does: every roster level, the farm, the standings, the payroll and any player in the league.
[*][b]New[/b] — Filters on Player Search. Position, bats, throws, an age range and a floor on playing time, all combinable, with a count of what is applied and one press to clear them. For a pitcher the position list becomes his role — starter, reliever, closer — and the playing-time box counts outs rather than plate appearances. Nine left-handed shortstops under 25 in this league, down from four hundred and sixty. A switch hitter answers to a search for either side, because he does bat left and leaving him out hides exactly the players a platoon question is about.
[*][b]Fixed[/b] — The increments-of-five setting now applies everywhere. It worked on the player card and nowhere else, so the depth chart, the farm system and both draft tables kept showing the raw grade and turning it on looked broken rather than partial. Sorting and every calculation still use the exact number, as before — a 57 still ranks above a 56 when both read 55.
[/list]
